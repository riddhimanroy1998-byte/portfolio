How the scoring works ›

Each candidate's score blends three signals into a single 0–100 number:

Must-have coverage (70%) — the share of weighted required skills the candidate matches. A missing must-have hurts far more than a missing nice-to-have.

Nice-to-have coverage (20%) — weighted bonus skills like SQL or workflow tools that separate strong candidates from adequate ones.

Experience fit (10%) — years against the role minimum, capped at 1.0 so over-experience isn't over-rewarded.

Only candidates at or above the threshold are shortlisted, so a recruiter reads a ranked few instead of the whole stack. The weights and bar are configurable per role; nothing is a black box.
