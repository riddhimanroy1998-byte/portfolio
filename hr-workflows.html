Leave & payroll preparation

The problem

Every leave request was logged by hand, and at month end the People team recalculated, per person, how many days each employee had taken, how many they were entitled to, and what paid balance remained. With 80 employees and several leave types, that was slow and error-prone exactly when payroll could not afford mistakes.

What I built

An approval-triggered system: a request submitted through a Notion form routes to the team lead, and on approval an n8n workflow logs it and updates a live per-person balance across paid, unpaid, paternity, and maternity leave. At month end, one action compiles every person's balances into a single CSV formatted for the payroll provider. Nobody checks the ledger by hand anymore.

The problem

French employers reimburse 50% of an employee's public-transit (Navigo) pass. Each month the three-person team blocked half a day to open every employee's pass, read it, and compute the payout by hand. It was not one-size-fits-all: students, interns, and standard employees carry different subscription types and rules.

What I built

A form where each employee submits their pass details once. An n8n workflow reads the submission, applies the correct rule for that employee's profile, computes the 50% reimbursement, and registers it for the pay run. The recurring half-day of manual checking dropped to zero.

For each open role it extracts the requirements, scores every applicant against them with a structured OpenAI API prompt returning a JSON match score, gates at 90%, and surfaces a ranked shortlist so the team selects rather than reads everything. High-confidence matches auto-surface; borderline cases route to a human. This one has its own interactive demo and write-up in the repo.
