How the prioritization works ›

Every brand is ranked by its trailing six-month GMV, pulled from the data warehouse. Coverage is measured as the share of total GMV that has complete compliance data, not the share of brands.

Because revenue follows a long tail, a handful of brands carry most of the exposure. Filling them first moves the needle far faster than working through the catalogue in order, which is why a small, ranked effort clears the regulatory bar in weeks rather than months.

In production this register fed an automated research step that filled most brands, with the obscure long tail left to a human by design. The same ranking doubled as a prioritization shield: a request to enrich brands sitting far below the cut could be answered with data, not opinion.
